# 30-Day Implementation Schedule

Primary 30-day goal: get 50-100 local leads and fill the first beginner classes.  
Second goal: turn first-time students into repeat students.  
Third goal: build local trust through reviews, real social proof, partnerships, and consistent bilingual content.

## Days 1-3: Foundation

Set up:
- Instagram profile
- Facebook page
- Google Business Profile
- WhatsApp Business
- Booking link or simple landing page
- Basic brand guide
- Lead tracking sheet

Create:
- Instagram bio
- Facebook description
- Google descriptions
- WhatsApp greeting
- First 10 captions
- First 10 story posts
- First flyer draft

Important correction:
Do not use "500+ students" or "5-star reviews" until they are real. Use "new studio," "opening June 20," "small groups," and "guided by Yareen."

## Days 4-7: Content Preparation

Create:
- 20 Reel ideas
- 10 static post designs
- 10 story templates
- FAQ post series
- What to expect post
- First Flight explanation post
- Opening announcement post

Prepare all high-priority content in Hebrew and Arabic.

## Week 2: Pre-Launch Awareness

Goal: make local people aware that Cloud & Core is opening.

Publish:
- Studio reveal post
- Yareen founder story
- What is aerial yoga?
- First Flight announcement
- Behind-the-scenes Reels
- Countdown stories
- Polls and Q&A stickers

Lead collection:
- Add every Instagram DM, phone inquiry, WhatsApp inquiry, and partner referral to the CRM tracker.
- Ask preferred class type and best days/times.

## Week 3: Booking Push

Goal: get first class bookings.

Actions:
- Announce First Flight booking
- Post schedule if ready
- Post pricing if ready
- If pricing is not ready, collect waitlist leads and class preference
- Run small local Instagram/Facebook ads
- Contact 20 local partners
- Invite people to the open house
- Send WhatsApp follow-up to interested leads

## Week 4: Launch Week

Goal: fill intro classes and open house.

Actions:
- Daily stories
- Open house promotion
- Yareen videos
- Studio setup videos
- Reminder messages
- Local partner reposts
- Launch offer deadline if price is finalized
- Collect reviews after real first classes
- Retarget people who engaged but did not book

## Weekly Review Routine

Every week, check:
- How many new leads came in?
- Which channel brought them?
- Which class type got the most interest?
- How many leads booked?
- How many attended?
- How many returned or asked about packages?
- Which posts produced DMs?
- Which ads produced bookings, not just likes?

## Price Decision Note

Because prices are not final, avoid posting "cheap" or "discount" language. Use:
- "Special launch price coming soon"
- "Join the update list"
- "Limited first beginner groups"
- "Ask for details"

Before finalizing prices, compare:
- Competitor/local activity prices
- Class capacity of 8-10
- Instructor time
- Rent/equipment costs
- Birthday/event prep time
- Desired profit per class
- Whether intro price encourages trial without training people to wait for discounts
