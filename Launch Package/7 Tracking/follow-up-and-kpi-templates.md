## Weekly KPI Review Questions

Ask these every week:

1. Which source brought the most real leads?
2. Which source brought actual bookings?
3. Which class type has strongest demand?
4. What stopped people from booking: price, schedule, uncertainty, fear, or booking friction?
5. Which post or Reel produced the most DMs?
6. Which ad produced the lowest cost per booking?
7. How many people attended and then asked about returning?
8. What should change next week?
