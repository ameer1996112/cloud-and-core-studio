# Cloud & Core Studio Brand Mini-Guide

## Studio Snapshot

Business name: Cloud & Core Studio  
Business type: local yoga and aerial yoga studio  
Launch location: Hurfish and Yanouh  
Opening date: June 20, 2026  
Audience: women, girls, mothers and daughters, kids, families booking birthdays, and local beginners who want a calm first experience  
Launch capacity: 8-10 students per class  
Instructor: Yareen  
Current proof level: new studio, no student testimonials yet

## Recommended Tone

Use a friendly-premium tone.

This is the best fit because the studio needs to feel elegant and special, but not cold or intimidating. The audience includes women, mothers, and kids, so the voice should be warm, simple, safe, and welcoming. Avoid sounding like an exclusive luxury spa or a spiritual retreat that may feel too distant from local families.

Tone words:
- Calm
- Warm
- Elegant
- Supportive
- Beginner-friendly
- Safe
- Personal
- Local

Do not use:
- Extreme fitness language
- Body-shaming
- Medical promises
- Fake social proof
- Pressure language

## Brand Positioning

Cloud & Core Studio is a calm, beginner-friendly yoga and aerial yoga studio for women and kids in Hurfish and Yanouh, created to help students feel lighter, stronger, and more confident through small classes, step-by-step guidance, and a safe welcoming atmosphere.

## Language Rules

Use Hebrew and Arabic equally in public-facing materials. Keep Cloud & Core Studio in English.

For small graphics, use Hebrew and Arabic only, with the English name as the logo/title. Avoid crowding three full languages into one visual.

Use right-to-left layout for Hebrew and Arabic. Give each language the same visual weight.

## Color Direction

Primary navy: #131920 or #384A5D  
Cream: #F8F6F1  
Soft beige: #B1A898  
Cloud blue: #D7E7EF  
White: #FFFFFF

Use navy for text and premium contrast. Use cream or white space as the main background. Use beige and cloud blue as soft accents. Avoid making the brand too dark for local materials; use lighter backgrounds for flyers and WhatsApp posts.

## Font Direction

English:
- Elegant serif for headlines, such as Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display
- Clean sans-serif for body text, such as Outfit, Inter, or Montserrat

Hebrew:
- Clean readable sans-serif, such as Assistant, Noto Sans Hebrew, Heebo, or Rubik

Arabic:
- Clean readable Arabic font, such as Noto Sans Arabic, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, or Cairo

Rule: readability is more important than decoration, especially for Arabic and Hebrew.

## Logo Usage

Use the logo with generous white space around it. Do not place it over busy photos. For social posts, place the logo small at the top or bottom, not as the main content unless the post is an announcement.

Preferred backgrounds:
- Cream
- Deep navy
- Soft beige
- Clean photo background with a soft overlay

Avoid:
- Neon colors
- Crowded layouts
- Logo stretching
- Heavy shadows
- Too many icons

## Instagram Visual Style

Use:
- Calm studio photography

- Yareen preparing the studio

- Text cards with minimal text
- Cream background, navy text, beige/blue accents

Post rhythm:
- 40% education and beginner reassurance
- 25% studio/opening/story

## Safety And Trust Notes

Use safety messaging often:
- Beginner-friendly
- Step-by-step guidance
- Small class sizes
- Supportive instructor
- No previous experience needed
- Comfortable clothing recommended
- Come 10 minutes early
- Tell Yareen before class about injuries, pregnancy, or medical concerns
