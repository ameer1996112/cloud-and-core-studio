# Local Partnership Plan

Goal: build local trust and collect warm leads before and during the launch month.

Partnership idea:
Cloud & Core gives the partner’s customers a first-class intro offer, and the partner receives visibility on Cloud & Core social media or a small referral benefit once pricing is finalized.

Recommended first target: 20 local partners before launch week.
