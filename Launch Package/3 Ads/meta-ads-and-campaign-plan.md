# Meta Ads And Campaign Plan

Recommended launch budget until prices are final: start small, test creative, then increase only after messages convert into real leads.

Suggested first test:
- 7 days
- 3 campaigns
- 2-3 creatives per campaign
- Local radius: 5-15 km around Hurfish and Yanouh
- Daily budget: start with a modest amount you are comfortable testing, then compare cost per WhatsApp message, cost per lead, and booking rate

## Campaign 1: Awareness

Objective: introduce Cloud & Core Studio to local women and families.

Audience:
- Women and parents near Hurfish and Yanouh
- Interests: yoga, Pilates, wellness, fitness, dance, meditation, self-care, kids activities, birthday ideas

Creative:
- Calm studio video
- Hammocks being prepared
- Yareen welcome video
- Logo with opening date

Message:
New women and kids yoga/aerial yoga studio opening June 20, 2026.

## Campaign 2: Leads / Bookings

Objective: get First Flight inquiries and class bookings.

Audience:
- Local women
- People who engaged with Instagram/Facebook
- People who watched launch videos
- Website visitors if pixel is installed

Creative:
- First Flight intro class video
- "No experience needed" graphic
- Yareen explaining the first class

Message:
Beginner-friendly First Flight class. No previous experience needed. Small groups.

## Campaign 3: Retargeting

Objective: remind interested people to book.

Audience:
- Instagram/Facebook engagers
- Video viewers
- Website visitors
- People who opened forms/messages but did not book

Creative:
- Limited small group spots
- Booking methods
- Opening week reminder

Message:
Small class sizes mean limited spots in beginner groups.

## Budget Suggestions

Use this only as a testing framework until prices are set.

Week 1:
- Spend mostly on awareness and video views.
- Goal: local recognition and engagement.

Week 2:
- Shift toward messages/leads.
- Goal: collect interested names and class preferences.

Week 3:
- Run First Flight booking push.
- Goal: convert leads into bookings.

Launch week:
- Retarget engagers and people who messaged.
- Goal: fill first groups and open house.

## Ad Testing Notes

Track:
- Cost per message
- Cost per lead
- Cost per booked First Flight
- Which class type gets most interest
- Hebrew vs Arabic response rate
- Yareen video vs graphic performance

Decision rule:
If an ad gets messages but no bookings, the issue may be price, schedule, or booking friction. If an ad gets low engagement, test a clearer hook or a stronger local visual.
