## Filming Rules

- Film in natural light when possible.
- Keep every video calm and clean.
- Avoid extreme poses for beginner posts.
- Do not show students without permission.
- For kids, get parent permission before filming or posting.
- Add captions/subtitles because many people watch without sound.
